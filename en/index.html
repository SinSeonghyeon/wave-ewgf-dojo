Mishima Dojo

A free, frame-accurate trainer for Tekken 8 EWGF and wave dash. Runs in your browser, nothing to install.

Open the trainer (English)

The trainer itself is a single page with Korean, English and Japanese UI. The button above opens it in English; you can switch languages in its settings at any time.

What it is

Mishima Dojo judges the two inputs every Mishima-style character in Tekken 8 lives on: the Electric Wind God Fist (EWGF, f,N,d,d/f+2, written 6N23+2 in the app) and the wave dash (crouch dash chains, 6N23 6 N 6N23 6 N …).

It records the time of every key or button press in milliseconds and grades it against one frame (16.67 ms). You do not need the game running. It is an unofficial fan-made tool, not a macro or a cheat: it reads inputs on a web page and never touches the game.

How judging works

  • EWGF: measures the gap between the moment d/f registers and the moment you press 2. Inside the window (0.5f, 0.7f or 0.9f; default 0.7f = ±12 ms) it is an EWGF. Late is a plain WGF, early is a fail, and either way you see the offset in ms so you know which way to adjust.
  • Wave dash: checks the f → N → d → d/f order, catches a missing cancel f after d/f and a missing neutral before the next dash, and counts chain length and dashes per second.
  • Precision: browser input timing is about 4 ms precise, so expect small differences from the real game. The trainer is for building the rhythm, the game is the final judge.

Timed modes and the weekly leaderboard

Besides free practice there are Wave 10s, EWGF ×20, Wave EWGF ×10, Dummy Rush 30s and Backdash 10s. Results are charted as a button-timing histogram and a wave speed trend.

Pick a nickname to post to the weekly leaderboard, compare with other players and get a tier (SS to D) from your top percentage. Result cards can be saved as images for sharing.

Controls

  • Keyboard: WASD for directions and U I J K for buttons 1 2 3 4 by default, all remappable.
  • Gamepad / hitbox / leverless: any controller the browser recognises as a gamepad. Remap directions and buttons in settings. 1P and 2P side are both supported.
  • Touch: phones get an on-screen pad and buttons, with lower precision.
  • Stick: untested by the author. If yours reads wrong, please get in touch.

FAQ

My EWGF keeps coming out as a normal WGF.
Your 2 is landing after d/f. Watch the ms offset in the result and press 2 as you roll from d into d/f, not after d/f is fully in.
My wave dash keeps breaking.
Usually the cancel f after d/f is missing, or that f is reused as the next dash's starting f so the neutral never registers. The result panel names the fault ("Missing cancel 6", "Missing start 6").
Is this a macro?
No. It only reads your inputs on this web page and never interacts with the game.
Where is my data stored?
Settings and records stay in your browser. Only leaderboard scores and nicknames go to the server.
Is it free?
Yes, with no ads. It runs on donations via Ko-fi.
